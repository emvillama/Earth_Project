# Final Stage — BIOSPHERE
**Goal:** The full vision — a seamless app paired with head-tracking headphones that
delivers a hyper-real, orientation-aware walk through multiple 3D biomes.

## Substages
- ☐ **F.1 Headphone head-tracking.** Integrate head-tracked spatial audio (AirPods/
  Atmos head-tracking or IMU headphones) so turning your *head* re-localizes the field.
- ☐ **F.2 HRTF & binaural quality.** High-quality (ideally personalized) HRTF, near-
  field cues, and occlusion so localization is convincing.
- ☐ **F.3 Multiple biomes.** Forest, wetland/river, coast, meadow, etc. — each with its
  own spawn tables and soundscape; seamless transitions between them.
- ☐ **F.4 Environmental realism.** Dynamic weather + time-of-day audio, distance/
  occlusion modeling, per-biome reverb.
- ☐ **F.5 Seamless, no-look UX.** Instant start, minimal calibration, guided or free
  "walks/journeys," graceful backgrounding.
- ☐ **F.6 Content & polish.** Curated field-recording-quality audio, final mixing,
  accessibility. Ship it.

**Exit criteria:** put on headphones, start the app, and take a believable audio walk
through at least two distinct biomes where head + body movement fully drive the world.
